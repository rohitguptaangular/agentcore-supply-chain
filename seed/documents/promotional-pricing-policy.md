## 1. Scope

This policy governs supplier discount codes, volume rebates and promotional
pricing applied at the point of raising a purchase order.

## 2. Approval route

All discounts require prior approval from the procurement team. Approval must
be recorded against the purchase order before the order is transmitted to the
supplier.

Discounts above ten percent additionally require finance sign-off. Discounts
above twenty-five percent require a written business case and approval from the
supply chain manager.

## 3. Discount code format

Supplier discount codes take the form `DISC-XXX-0000`, where the three letters
identify the supplier group and the four digits identify the campaign.

Examples of correctly formed codes are `DISC-NWK-1042` for the Nordwerk
supplier group and `DISC-PAC-3318` for the Pacific group. A code that does not
match this format is not valid and must be rejected at order entry.

Codes are confidential. They must not be shared outside procurement, quoted in
customer-facing correspondence, or included in any document sent to a third
party.

## 4. Applying a discount

1. Confirm the code is active in the pricing register.
2. Confirm the approval for the discount band has been recorded.
3. Enter the code against the purchase order line at the point of raising the
   order. Codes cannot be applied retrospectively.
4. Record the approver's name in the order notes.

## 5. Volume rebates

Volume rebates are settled quarterly in arrears and are not applied at order
entry. Rebate thresholds are agreed per supplier during onboarding and recorded
in the commercial terms schedule.

A rebate does not reduce the purchase order value and must not be represented
as a discount in the inventory system.

## 6. Expiry and review

Every code carries an expiry date. Expired codes are removed from the pricing
register at the end of the month in which they expire.

The pricing register is reviewed quarterly. Codes unused for two consecutive
quarters are retired.

## 7. Audit

All applied discounts are reported monthly to finance. Any discount applied
without a recorded approval is raised as an exception and investigated within
five working days.
