# Supplier Onboarding Manual

Document reference: PROC-SOM-002
Revision: 4
Owner: Procurement

These procedures are fictional and exist only to give the knowledge base
realistic material to retrieve.

## 1. Purpose

This manual describes how a new supplier is assessed, approved and added to the
approved supplier register, and how existing suppliers are reviewed.

## 2. Onboarding stages

Onboarding has four stages and a supplier may not skip any of them.

1. **Qualification** — commercial and legal screening
2. **Technical assessment** — capability and sample evaluation
3. **Audit** — on-site or remote audit against our quality requirements
4. **Registration** — addition to the approved supplier register

A supplier is not permitted to receive a purchase order until registration is
complete.

## 3. Qualification requirements

The supplier must provide:

- Certificate of incorporation and current trading address
- Evidence of public liability and product liability insurance
- Most recent two years of financial statements
- A completed conflict of interest declaration
- Named quality and commercial contacts

Suppliers who cannot provide two years of financial statements may still
proceed where a parent company guarantee is offered.

## 4. Certification requirements

All suppliers must hold ISO 9001 certification, valid at the point of
registration and maintained thereafter.

Suppliers of electrical components must additionally demonstrate compliance
with IEC conductor standards. Suppliers of aerospace-adjacent mechanical parts
must hold AS9100.

Environmental certification, ISO 14001, is required for suppliers whose annual
order value exceeds two hundred thousand units of currency. A lapsed
environmental certification places the supplier in the action required state
and blocks new orders until it is restored.

## 5. Technical assessment

Request first article samples for every part number to be supplied. Samples are
inspected against the applicable quality standard for the product category.

A supplier failing first article inspection may submit one further set of
samples. A second failure ends the onboarding process for that part number.

## 6. Lead time and performance commitments

Each supplier commits to a lead time per part number, recorded against the
supplier record and used by the replenishment calculation.

Performance rating is scored out of five and combines:

- On-time delivery, weighted fifty percent
- Quality acceptance rate, weighted thirty percent
- Responsiveness to queries, weighted twenty percent

A rating below 3.5 for two consecutive quarters triggers a formal performance
review. A rating below 3.0 suspends the supplier from new orders.

## 7. Ongoing audit cycle

Approved suppliers are audited every twelve months. Suppliers in the action
required state are audited within ninety days of the finding being raised.

Audit outcomes are recorded in the compliance register against the supplier
identifier, with the next audit date set at the point the audit closes.

## 8. Dual sourcing

Any product representing more than ten percent of category spend must have at
least two approved suppliers. Where a single source is unavoidable, a risk
waiver must be recorded and reviewed every six months.
